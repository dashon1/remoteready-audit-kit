## 📋 What's Inside

The **RemoteReady Audit Kit** is a professional PDF workbook containing structured checklists and interactive tools for managing labor law compliance in distributed work environments:

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Stress Check-In** | Assess team and individual stress levels to flag burnout risks early |
| **Energy Map** | Visualize energy patterns across your distributed workforce |
| **What to Stop / Delay / Delegate** | Prioritization framework for reducing overload and improving delegation |
| **Recovery Menu** | Curated recovery strategies aligned with labor wellness standards |
| **Support Script** | Ready-to-use communication templates for compliance conversations |
| **Weekly Commitments** | Trackable weekly goals to maintain ongoing compliance momentum |
