Frequently Asked Questions

Is this actual legal advice, or just a template?
This kit is an educational resource and professionally-researched template — not legal advice and not a substitute for an attorney. The policy template and checklists are based on commonly recognized best practices and publicly available state labor law guidance as of 2026. For company-specific situations involving significant legal risk, you should consult a licensed employment attorney in the relevant states. That said, this kit will give you a strong working foundation and help you have a much more productive conversation with any attorney you do engage.
My company has employees in 3 states. Does this cover all of them?
The Pro Kit's State-by-State Compliance Checklist covers the 10 states with the highest remote worker populations: California, Texas, New York, Florida, Washington, Illinois, Colorado, Massachusetts, Georgia, and Arizona. These 10 states account for roughly 68% of the US remote workforce. If your employees are in other states, the general remote work policy template and onboarding checklist apply everywhere — you'd just need to research the specific notice requirements for any state not covered in the checklist.
Can I use the policy template as-is, or do I need to customize it?
You'll need to fill in the bracketed fields with your company-specific details — things like your company name, your specific equipment provision policy, your reimbursement dollar thresholds, and your IT security requirements. The document is clearly marked with [COMPANY NAME], [INSERT POLICY], and similar placeholders wherever customization is required. The policy language itself is ready to use and covers all major categories. Most users complete the customization in under an hour.
Is this useful if my whole team is already remote and has been for years?
Absolutely — in fact, established remote teams are often at higher risk than newly remote ones because their informal arrangements have never been documented. If you don't have a written remote work policy on file, you don't have a policy. This kit helps you formalize what you're already doing, identify gaps you may have gotten used to living with, and onboard future hires with a consistent process. The audit workbook alone surfaces issues that most teams have been ignoring for years.
What format are the documents in? Can I edit them?
The Audit Workbook is a print-ready PDF. The three documents — the policy template, state checklist, and onboarding checklist — are delivered in Markdown format, which opens in any text editor, Notion, Obsidian, Google Docs (via import), or Word. Markdown is intentionally chosen so you own an editable, plain-text version of every document with no proprietary format lock-in. If you want a Word (.docx) version of any document, just email us and we'll convert it for you at no charge.
What if laws change after I buy this? Will the documents be outdated?
Remote work law is evolving rapidly — new state notice requirements, expense reimbursement rules, and tax nexus thresholds are being enacted every year. Basic Kit purchasers get the documents as of the date of purchase. Pro Kit purchasers get free updates for 12 months — whenever we update the state compliance checklist or policy template to reflect new legislation, you'll receive the updated versions automatically. This is particularly important for California, Colorado, and Illinois, which update their remote work rules most frequently.
