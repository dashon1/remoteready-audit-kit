## Purpose

This Remote Work Policy establishes the framework under which [COMPANY NAME] employees may work remotely on a full-time, part-time, or hybrid basis. It defines eligibility requirements, operational expectations, security obligations, and the rights and responsibilities of both the company and its remote employees. This policy applies to all employees approved for remote work arrangements, regardless of the state in which they work.

---

## 1. Eligibility Criteria

### 1.1 Who May Work Remotely

Remote work arrangements are available to employees who meet all of the following criteria:

- Have been employed with [COMPANY NAME] for a minimum of [90 days / 6 months] and have received at least one formal performance review
- Hold a position that [COMPANY NAME] has designated as remote-eligible (see Appendix A for the list of eligible roles)
- Have demonstrated the ability to perform their duties independently without day-to-day in-person supervision
- Have not received a formal written warning within the preceding [12 months]
- Maintain a home office or alternative work location that meets the requirements described in Section 3 of this policy

### 1.2 Approval Process

Remote work arrangements must be approved in writing by the employee's direct manager and countersigned by [HR DIRECTOR / PEOPLE OPERATIONS]. Approval is not automatic and may be revoked at any time for cause, including failure to meet performance standards or failure to comply with this policy. Employees must submit a Remote Work Request Form at least [10 business days] before the intended start date.

### 1.3 Non-Eligibility

The following employee categories are not eligible for remote work under this policy without explicit written approval from the CEO/President: employees on a performance improvement plan (PIP), employees in roles requiring physical presence (e.g., on-site client services, warehouse, reception), and employees in their initial [90-day / 6-month] onboarding period.

## 3. Home Office Requirements

### 3.1 Dedicated Workspace

Remote employees must maintain a dedicated workspace that is used primarily for work purposes. This space must have a door or other means of providing privacy during sensitive calls, videoconferences, or work involving confidential information. Working from shared public spaces (coffee shops, libraries, co-working spaces) is permitted [WITH / WITHOUT] prior manager approval, subject to the security requirements in Section 4.

### 3.2 Ergonomics and Safety

[COMPANY NAME] encourages employees to set up their workspace in accordance with basic ergonomic principles to reduce the risk of repetitive strain injuries. The company [WILL / WILL NOT] provide an ergonomic equipment allowance of up to [$___] for home office setup. Employees working from home are subject to the same OSHA general duty clause obligations as office workers; the employee's home workspace is considered a work location for purposes of work-related injury reporting.

### 3.3 Location Disclosure

Employees must notify [HR / PEOPLE OPERATIONS] of their primary remote work address, including state and country. Employees who relocate to a different state — even temporarily for periods exceeding [30 days] — must notify HR in writing at least [10 business days] before the relocation date. Failure to notify HR of a state change may create unintended tax and labor law obligations for the company and for the employee personally.

## 4. Home Office Security Requirements

### 4.1 Network Security

Remote employees must connect to all company systems through the company-provided VPN at all times when accessing company data, email, applications, or internal systems. Connecting to company systems over unsecured public Wi-Fi without VPN is strictly prohibited. Employees' home Wi-Fi networks must be secured with WPA2 or WPA3 encryption and protected by a strong password that is not shared with non-household members.

### 4.2 Device Security

All devices used for company work — whether company-provided or personal — must have:

- Full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker for Windows, FileVault for macOS)
- Automatic screen lock after no more than [5 minutes] of inactivity
- A device-level password or PIN separate from the user's login credentials
- All operating system and application security patches applied within [72 hours] of release
- Company-approved antivirus/endpoint protection software installed and active

### 4.3 Password and Access Management

Employees must use strong, unique passwords for all company accounts and must not reuse passwords across company and personal accounts. [COMPANY NAME] requires the use of [PASSWORD MANAGER TOOL] for all company credentials. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company systems, email, cloud storage, and any application containing customer or financial data.

### 4.4 Physical Security

Employees must ensure that confidential company information — including documents, screens, and conversations — is not visible or audible to household members or visitors. Printed company documents must be stored securely and shredded when no longer needed. Employees should use a privacy screen filter on their monitor when working in locations where screens may be visible to others.

### 4.5 Incidents and Breaches

Any suspected security incident, data breach, unauthorized access, or loss of a device containing company data must be reported to [IT SECURITY CONTACT] immediately and no later than [2 hours] after discovery. Employees who delay reporting security incidents in violation of this policy may be subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 5. Availability and Communication Expectations

### 5.1 Core Hours

All remote employees are expected to be available and responsive during the company's core working hours of [START TIME] to [END TIME] [TIME ZONE], Monday through Friday, unless a flexible schedule has been explicitly approved in writing by their manager. "Available" means reachable by Slack/Teams within [15 minutes] and by phone within [30 minutes] during core hours.

### 5.2 Flexible Arrangements

[COMPANY NAME] [DOES / DOES NOT] support fully flexible scheduling for certain roles. Where flexible scheduling is approved, the employee and manager must agree in writing on the employee's committed working hours and communicate those hours to teammates. Flexible schedules may be revoked with [2 weeks] written notice if business needs change.

### 5.3 Meeting Participation

Remote employees are expected to have their video camera on during company-wide meetings, one-on-ones with their manager, and client-facing calls unless explicitly excused. Requests to regularly work without camera on must be made in writing to the employee's manager.

### 5.4 Response Time Standards

Remote employees are expected to acknowledge direct messages in [SLACK / TEAMS] within [30 minutes] during core hours and respond to email within [4 hours] on the same business day. Employees who will be unavailable or delayed for any extended period during core hours must proactively communicate their status in [TEAM CHANNEL / to their manager].

### 5.5 Right to Disconnect

[COMPANY NAME] respects employees' right to disconnect outside of core working hours. Employees are not expected to respond to non-emergency communications after [END TIME] or on weekends, unless their role explicitly requires on-call availability and that obligation is documented in their job description or a separate on-call agreement.

## 7. Performance Measurement

### 7.1 Output-Based Evaluation

Remote employees are evaluated on the quality and timeliness of their work outputs, not on hours logged or time spent at a desk. Managers are expected to set clear, measurable quarterly objectives for all remote employees and evaluate performance against those objectives.

### 7.2 Check-In Schedule

All remote employees will have a minimum of [one 30-minute 1:1 with their manager per week] and a formal performance review [quarterly / semi-annually / annually] using the company's standard review process. Additional check-ins may be scheduled at manager discretion.

### 7.3 Performance Concerns

If a remote employee's performance falls below expectations, the manager will initiate a documented conversation within [10 business days] of identifying the issue. Persistent performance concerns will be addressed through the company's standard performance improvement process. Remote work status may be suspended or revoked as part of a performance improvement plan.

## 8. Confidentiality Obligations

### 8.1 Company Information

Remote employees have the same confidentiality obligations as on-site employees and must protect company information, client data, and proprietary materials at all times. The duty of confidentiality applies to verbal conversations, written communications, digital files, and physical documents. Employees must not discuss confidential company business in locations where it may be overheard by household members, guests, or other third parties.

### 8.2 Client and Customer Data

Employees who handle personally identifiable information (PII), protected health information (PHI), or financial data in their role must comply with all applicable data protection laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA where relevant) and [COMPANY NAME]'s Data Handling Policy. Customer records, financial data, and client communications must be accessed only through company-approved systems and must never be stored locally on personal devices.

### 8.3 Third-Party Confidentiality

Employees must not discuss any client, vendor, or partner's confidential information in the presence of individuals who do not have a business need to know, including family members. The confidentiality provisions of the employee's offer letter, employment agreement, and any NDAs executed with clients remain fully in effect for remote work.

## 9. Policy Violation Consequences

### 9.1 Disciplinary Framework

Violations of this Remote Work Policy will be addressed through [COMPANY NAME]'s standard disciplinary process, which includes verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, and termination. The severity of the disciplinary response will be proportionate to the nature and impact of the violation, the employee's history, and whether the violation was inadvertent or willful.

### 9.2 Immediate Suspension of Remote Privileges

[COMPANY NAME] reserves the right to immediately suspend an employee's remote work authorization — without prior notice — in cases involving: a confirmed or suspected data security breach, failure to comply with VPN or device security requirements, working from an unauthorized state or country without HR notification, or conduct that creates legal, financial, or reputational risk for the company.

### 9.3 Termination-Level Violations

The following violations may result in immediate termination without a prior warning: deliberate exposure of confidential company or client data, gross negligence resulting in a data breach, falsifying remote work time or availability records, and unauthorized access to company systems by third parties due to the employee's failure to follow security protocols.

### 9.4 Acknowledgment

All employees approved for remote work must sign and return a Remote Work Policy Acknowledgment Form confirming they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this policy. A copy of the signed acknowledgment will be retained in the employee's personnel file.
