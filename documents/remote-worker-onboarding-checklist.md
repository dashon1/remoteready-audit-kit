# Remote Worker Onboarding Checklist

**RemoteReady Audit Kit — 2026 Edition**

A 50-point checklist for onboarding new remote employees. Organized by timeline so nothing falls through the cracks — whether you're onboarding your first remote hire or your fiftieth.

**How to use this checklist:**
- Assign each section to the responsible party (HR, IT, Manager, or the New Hire themselves)
- Complete each item by the target date and check it off
- File the completed checklist in the employee's personnel record

---

## Section 1: Before Day 1

*These items must be completed before the employee's first day. Owner: HR + IT + Hiring Manager*

### Equipment & Technology

- [ ] **1. Equipment ordered and shipped** — Laptop, monitor, keyboard/mouse, headset ordered at least 10 business days before start date; tracking number sent to new hire
- [ ] **2. Equipment delivery confirmed** — New hire confirms receipt of all shipped equipment at least 2 days before start date
- [ ] **3. Accounts provisioned** — Email, Slack/Teams, project management tool, HR system, and all role-specific software accounts created
- [ ] **4. VPN access configured** — VPN credentials and setup instructions sent to new hire with a test they can run before Day 1
- [ ] **5. Password manager access granted** — New hire added to company password manager with initial credentials
- [ ] **6. MFA enrolled** — Multi-factor authentication set up for email, SSO, and all critical systems before Day 1
- [ ] **7. IT setup guide sent** — Step-by-step device configuration guide emailed (includes company security software installation, screen lock settings, disk encryption)

### Paperwork & Compliance

- [ ] **8. Offer letter countersigned and filed** — Fully executed copy in employee personnel file
- [ ] **9. I-9 remote verification completed** — I-9 Section 1 completed by employee; Section 2 completed via authorized remote verification agent or video-based process per current USCIS guidance
- [ ] **10. State-specific new hire forms completed** — State income tax withholding form (W-4 equivalent), state-specific disclosure forms, and new hire reporting submitted to state agency
- [ ] **11. Direct deposit form received and processed**
- [ ] **12. Remote Work Policy signed** — Employee has signed the Remote Work Policy Acknowledgment and copy is filed in their personnel record
- [ ] **13. NDA / confidentiality agreement executed** (if applicable)
- [ ] **14. Benefits enrollment completed or window opened** — Health, dental, vision, 401(k) election materials sent; enrollment deadline communicated

### Welcome & Logistics

- [ ] **15. Welcome email sent from HR** — Includes start time, who to contact, first day agenda, video call link for Day 1 orientation
- [ ] **16. Welcome message from manager sent** — Personal note from hiring manager setting tone, expressing excitement, sharing what the first week looks like
- [ ] **17. Team introduced via Slack/email** — Brief intro sent to team channel so teammates know who is starting and when
- [ ] **18. Day 1 calendar blocked and invites sent** — Orientation call, manager 1:1, and any team intro calls on calendar before Day 1

---

## Section 2: Day 1

*The first day should be structured, warm, and focused on getting the new hire oriented — not overwhelmed with information.*

### Setup & Orientation

- [ ] **19. IT orientation call completed** — 30-minute call to confirm all equipment is working, VPN connects, all apps are accessible; troubleshoot any issues same day
- [ ] **20. HR orientation session completed** — Review: benefits, PTO policy, remote work policy, communication expectations, expense reimbursement process, org chart
- [ ] **21. Manager 1:1 — Day 1 welcome** — 45–60 minute call: context on the team, what the first 30 days will look like, key priorities, how the manager prefers to work
- [ ] **22. Core tools walkthrough** — Walk new hire through the 3–5 tools they'll use every day: project management, communication, file storage, time tracking (if applicable)
- [ ] **23. Team intro calls scheduled** — Calendar holds placed for 20-minute intro calls with each direct teammate within the first week

### Access Confirmation

- [ ] **24. All accounts verified working** — New hire logs into each required system and confirms access; any access issues escalated to IT same day
- [ ] **25. File storage access confirmed** — New hire can access shared drives/Notion/Confluence and finds onboarding materials
- [ ] **26. Calendar and video conferencing working** — Test video call completed to confirm camera, mic, and screen share function

---

## Section 3: Week 1

*Week 1 is about context, relationships, and starting to understand the work. Keep training sessions to 60–90 minutes max to avoid remote-onboarding fatigue.*

### Training & Context

- [ ] **27. Company overview session** — Founding story, mission, business model, current priorities, key metrics, org structure; 60-minute session with HR or a senior leader
- [ ] **28. Role-specific training started** — Formal training for the new hire's function begins; materials, recorded sessions, or live walkthroughs provided
- [ ] **29. Product/service deep dive** — New hire understands what the company sells, who the customers are, and how their role connects to customer outcomes
- [ ] **30. Key processes documented and reviewed** — New hire walks through the 3–5 core processes in their role; SOPs shared and reviewed together with manager or peer

### Relationships

- [ ] **31. All team intro calls completed** — 20-minute 1:1s with every direct teammate; manager to facilitate introductions and provide context before each call
- [ ] **32. Cross-functional intro calls scheduled** — If the role requires working with other departments (sales, engineering, finance), intro calls set up during Week 1 or 2
- [ ] **33. Buddy/mentor assigned** — New hire has a designated go-to person for day-to-day questions (separate from their manager); first check-in with buddy completed

### Expectations & Tools

- [ ] **34. OKRs or 30-60-90 plan shared** — New hire receives written goals for their first 30, 60, and 90 days; reviewed and discussed with manager
- [ ] **35. Meeting cadence established** — Recurring 1:1 with manager, team standup, and any required weekly meetings added to calendar
- [ ] **36. Communication norms documented** — New hire understands: response time expectations, when to use Slack vs. email vs. video call, core hours, and how to indicate availability
- [ ] **37. Time zone and availability documented** — New hire's working hours logged in team calendar or company directory; teammates notified of any time zone differences

---

## Section 4: Month 1

*The goal of Month 1 is integration: the new hire should be producing real work, building real relationships, and feeling genuinely part of the team.*

### Check-ins & Feedback

- [ ] **38. Week 2 manager check-in** — 30-minute structured check-in: what's going well, what's confusing, what help is needed; manager takes notes and follows up on blockers
- [ ] **39. 30-day formal review** — Structured review against 30-day goals: areas of strength, areas for development, clear expectations for Days 31–60; documented in writing
- [ ] **40. New hire sentiment check** — HR or manager asks directly: "What's one thing going well and one thing that could be better?" Track responses to improve onboarding over time
- [ ] **41. Benefits enrollment deadline confirmed** — New hire has completed benefits elections; any questions about coverage answered before the enrollment window closes

### Integration

- [ ] **42. First real project or deliverable completed** — New hire has shipped something meaningful: a document, a design, a plan, a completed task — something concrete that is part of actual work
- [ ] **43. First solo meeting or call run** — New hire has led at least one call or meeting independently (or participated with a path to leading one in Week 5–6)
- [ ] **44. Process gaps documented** — New hire has been invited to note anything in their onboarding that was unclear, missing, or could be improved; feedback shared with HR
- [ ] **45. Cross-team relationships building** — New hire has had at least one substantive conversation with a colleague outside their direct team
- [ ] **46. Expense reimbursement submitted** — Any reimbursable expenses from the first month (equipment, internet, office supplies) submitted through the expense system

### Compliance Wrap-Up

- [ ] **47. State registration confirmed** — HR confirms company has registered for payroll taxes in the state where the new hire is working (if a new state for the company)
- [ ] **48. All signed documents filed** — Remote Work Policy, NDA, I-9, state new hire forms — all executed and filed in personnel system
- [ ] **49. Equipment asset tag logged** — Any company equipment in the new hire's possession is logged in the company's asset management system with the employee's name and address
- [ ] **50. 90-day goals set** — New hire and manager have agreed on written goals for the 30-to-90-day window; new hire knows what success looks like at the 3-month mark
